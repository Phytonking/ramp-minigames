Ramp Agent Cards (Ramp Express): Ramp issues scoped, single-purpose agent cards that can only be used for the exact charge they were created for. As an agent, you collect the legitimate scoped charges that match your card while dodging everything that doesn't belong: open (unscoped) cards, duplicate charges, fake/fraudulent vendors, and expired authorization tokens. Grab only what your card is scoped for; touching anything off-scope blows your budget.
